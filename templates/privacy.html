Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SaWings ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using SaWings, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly when creating an account and using the Service, including your name, email address, and password. We also collect the expense data you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate the Service — authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating summaries and reports for you. We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database for as long as your account remains active. Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing and are never kept in plain text. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. Questions you ask the Advisor, and its answers, are stored with your account in the same way.

4. Third Party Services

SaWings does not share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting and font services) that are necessary to operate the Service; these providers do not have access to your expense data.

If you choose "Suggest Importance and Dependencies" on a Goal, that Goal's name, target amount and Deadline, and the names, targets and Deadlines of your other Goals, are sent to an AI provider (Anthropic) to produce the suggestion. This only happens when you click the button, and you always review a suggestion before saving it.

If you ask the Advisor a question, your question is sent to an AI provider (OpenAI) together with your Income, your Obligations (names and amounts), your Goals (names, target amounts, Saved Amounts, Deadlines and Importance), your Top-ups and your Savings Pot amounts, so it can answer. This only happens when you ask, and nothing is saved until you confirm the changes it proposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
